Where to stay in Tokyo

Choose a base around your daily journeys.

Map your repeated journeys

List your likely outings and arrival point. Compare accommodation by practical access to those places, rather than relying on a single universal best-neighborhood recommendation.

Read beyond the headline price

Check room dimensions, bed configuration, cancellation terms, and baggage arrangements. Families should verify occupancy rules for their exact party.

Test the location

Look up the route from the relevant station exit to the property. Consider luggage, late arrival, and whether the route suits everyone in your group.
