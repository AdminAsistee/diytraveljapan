Booked on another platform?

Use the original seller’s booking details for changes, cancellation and refund status. We cannot access or modify another platform’s reservations.

Klook ↗Booking.com ↗Airbnb ↗

Have these details ready

  • Booking provider and booking reference
  • Activity name, visit date and timezone
  • What happened and the resolution you’re requesting
  • Relevant policy or confirmation screenshots, with sensitive details hidden

Do not send card numbers, passwords, passport scans or API keys. This page does not submit a support ticket; email and phone links open your own apps.

Waiting for a refund?

Ask the original seller to distinguish: request received, cancellation accepted, refund approved, and refund sent. Ask for the amount, currency, destination and expected arrival date. Those stages are different; only the seller/payment provider can confirm the status.

Payment is not the same as a usable ticket

Check whether the booking is pending or confirmed, then find the actual voucher and entry instructions. If a deadline is approaching and confirmation is missing, contact the seller before buying again.
